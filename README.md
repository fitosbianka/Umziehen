# Umziehen

Planning our move for Noah's KFO specialization, with three possible futures: Bern, Zürich or Basel.

* **Interactive map**: https://claude.ai/artifact/3APp6rKyhSLPgrPFYcBeeF with all three scenarios, the candidate places, commute times for both of us, rent ranges and the strategy for each case. The source of the map is `index.html` in this repository.
* **Full written plan**: [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) with the reasoning, the money picture, the Fribourg question and the step by step checklist.

Quick orientation: in the Bern case we stay in the green belt south of the city, Kehrsatz or Spiegel first. In the Zürich case we move to the middle, Aarau or Olten. In the Basel case Olten again, or Gundeldingen behind the station for city years. Olten is the one address that works for both away scenarios at once.

Commute minutes are door to door estimates, rents are rough asking ranges. Check SBB and current listings before trusting any single number.
